how we got here

We kept meeting owners who had been sold tools without a diagnosis.

Between us we've spent more than thirty years in consulting, operations, and marketing, much of it inside larger companies with the budgets to hire a strategist, a developer, and an agency separately. Small businesses can't, so they end up with a website from one vendor, ads from another, and a stack of software subscriptions nobody connected.

Zoley was built to do the diagnosis first. We audit how a business actually runs, rank what's costing it the most, and then build the fix ourselves, inside the tools the owner already has. When the research and the build come from the same people, the result gets measured against the plan instead of drifting away from it.

We don't sell retainers you can't cancel, software we get commission on, or reports nobody reads. Every engagement is scoped in writing, priced before it starts, and handed over with documentation so you own what we built.

Every engagement follows the same four stages.
