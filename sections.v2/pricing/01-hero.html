how pricing works

Priced per engagement. Agreed in writing before we start.

We don't publish a rate card, because the honest answer to "what does it cost?" depends on what needs to be built. A pricing review and a full automation build are different amounts of work, and pretending otherwise would mean overcharging one client to subsidize another.

What we do publish is the process. Every engagement is scoped to specific deliverables with a fixed price, set before any work begins. No hourly meters, no retainers you can't cancel, and no invoice for something you didn't agree to.
