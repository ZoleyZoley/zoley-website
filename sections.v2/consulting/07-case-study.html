A local business pricing entirely on instinct.

The owner had set prices at launch based on what felt reasonable and hadn't revisited them in three years. We benchmarked eight competitors, interviewed a dozen recent customers about what they'd compared and what they'd have paid, and found both core offers sitting well below the band the market already accepted. We restructured the two offers, wrote the messaging for the change, and sequenced the rollout so existing customers were handled first. Revenue rose by more than a quarter without a single additional customer.
