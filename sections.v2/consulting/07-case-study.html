forecast yearly revenue, from about $300k to about $750k in four months

A physical therapy practice charging far less than the work was worth.

The practice ran on a single option at $150 with nothing above it, priced years earlier and never revisited. We benchmarked the local market and found the ceiling sat well above what the owner believed it was, so we built a three-tier structure: the $150 option stayed exactly where it was, and two new tiers went in above it at $350 and $500 a month. The messaging pointed new patients at the upper tiers while existing ones kept the price they signed up for. Four months on, the forecast for the year had moved from about $300,000 to about $750,000.
