what you actually receive

Three documents, written to be used.

Every strategy engagement ends with the same three deliverables. They're short on purpose. A plan you can hold in your head is a plan that gets executed.

01

The findings brief

What we learned about your customers, competitors, and pricing, with the evidence behind each conclusion.

02

The recommendation

The decision we'd make in your position and why, with the two or three alternatives we ruled out and what it would take to change our mind.

03

The 90-day roadmap

Sequenced actions with owners, costs, and the metric each one should move. Built so you can execute it with or without us.
