Six questions we answer with evidence.

Each engagement is scoped around one or two of these. The output is a decision you can defend, not a report that sits in a drawer.

1

Market research

Who actually buys, how they decide, what they'd switch for. Built from customer interviews and demand data, not survey guesswork.

"Is there enough demand here to be worth pursuing?"
2

Competitor analysis

A structured read of who you're up against: their offers, pricing, positioning, and the gaps they're leaving open.

"Where can we win without a price war?"
3

Pricing strategy

What the market will bear, how to structure tiers, and where you're currently leaving margin on the table or pricing yourself out.

"Are we charging the right amount, the right way?"
4

New offer development

Identifying the product or service line adjacent to what you already do that your customers would buy tomorrow.

"What should we sell next?"
5

Positioning & messaging

A clear statement of who you're for, what you do differently, and why it matters, tested against how buyers actually talk.

"Why should someone pick us?"
6

Growth roadmap

The research converted into a sequenced plan: what to do first, what it costs, and what result to expect at each stage.

"What do we do Monday morning?"
