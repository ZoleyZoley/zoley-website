What's included when you build with us.

A launch engagement is scoped around the pieces you actually need. Most founders take three or four of these. Each one ends with something you can hold.

Strategy sessions

Structured working sessions to turn the idea into a business model: who it's for, what it costs to deliver, how it makes money, and what has to be true for it to work.

You get: a one-page business model and a 90-day plan

Market research

Evidence that people will pay: demand signals, competitor pricing, and interviews with the customers you're planning to serve.

You get: a findings brief with a go / adjust / stop recommendation

Tool recommendations & setup

The right payments, scheduling, invoicing, and CRM tools for your model, selected independently and configured to work together from day one.

You get: a working stack, documented

Website build

A fast, clear site that says what you do, who it's for, and how to buy, on a platform you can edit yourself.

You get: a live site with tracking, on a domain you own

Quick-start templates

Proposal, pricing sheet, intake form, and outreach templates written for your business so the first sale doesn't wait on paperwork.

You get: a set of editable documents ready to send

Logo & brand basics

A clean visual identity: logo, color, type, and the few rules needed to keep everything looking like it came from the same company.

You get: logo files and a one-page brand guide
