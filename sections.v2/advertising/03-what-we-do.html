What a managed campaign includes.

Advertising works as a system or not at all. Each part below is included because the campaign underperforms without it.

1

Audience & targeting

Built from who has actually bought from you, not from platform defaults. Geography, intent, and timing set so the ad reaches people in a buying frame of mind.

2

Search & social campaigns

Google for people already looking, Meta and others for people who fit the profile but haven't searched yet. We recommend the channel the numbers support, not both by default.

3

Landing pages

A dedicated page per campaign, matched to the ad, with one action. Sending paid traffic to a homepage is the most common way to waste it.

4

Retargeting

Follow-up ads for people who visited and didn't act, sequenced so they see a reason to return rather than the same ad again.

5

Testing & optimization

Structured tests on headlines, offers, and audiences, with a decision rule agreed in advance so results turn into changes instead of debates.

6

Tracking & reporting

Conversion tracking configured from day one, and a monthly report in plain language: what we spent, what it produced, what we're changing next.
