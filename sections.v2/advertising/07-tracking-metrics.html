tracking & reporting

Six numbers, reported monthly, in plain language.

Ad platforms will show you dozens of metrics. Most of them are there to make the platform look good. We report on the handful that connect spend to revenue, and we explain what each one means for the decision in front of you.

You'll always be able to answer one question from the report: for every dollar we put in, what came back?

Total spend

What went out, by channel and campaign, against the budget we agreed.

Cost per click

What each visit cost. Useful for spotting audience or bidding problems early.

Click-through rate

How often people who saw the ad acted on it. A read on whether the message lands.

Cost per lead

What each inquiry cost. The number the whole campaign is managed against.

Conversion rate

The share of visitors who became leads. The clearest signal on the landing page.

Return on ad spend

Revenue attributed to the campaign divided by what it cost. The number that decides whether we scale.
