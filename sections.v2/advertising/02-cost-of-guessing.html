The cost of running ads without a system

Boosted posts. Untracked clicks. A budget that just disappears.

Most small-business advertising fails in a predictable way: the audience is too broad, the ad sends people to the homepage, and nobody can say which clicks turned into customers. The platform reports impressions and clicks, which feel like progress and aren't. We build campaigns the other way around: decide what a lead is worth, set up tracking to count them, and only then spend.
