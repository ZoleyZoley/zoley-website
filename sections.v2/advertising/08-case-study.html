A local business spending every month with no idea what it bought.

The previous setup was a broad-radius campaign sending clicks to the homepage, with no conversion tracking. We started by establishing what a customer was worth, then rebuilt the audience around past buyers, wrote a dedicated landing page with a single action, installed tracking, and routed every lead into an instant follow-up sequence. The budget didn't change. Cost per lead fell by roughly two thirds, and for the first time the owner could see which ads produced revenue.
