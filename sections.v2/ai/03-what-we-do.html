What we deploy, and the job each one does.

Each of these is scoped as a specific system with a defined owner, a defined input, and a defined output. If it can't be described that way, we don't build it.

1

Tool selection & vetting

An independent recommendation on which AI tools fit your workflow, data, and budget. We don't resell software, so there's no incentive to steer you.

2

Customer-facing assistant

Answers pricing, availability, and policy questions on your site or by text, trained on your actual answers, and hands off to a person when it should.

3

Content & correspondence drafting

First drafts of proposals, follow-up emails, and social posts in your voice, queued for your review rather than sent blind.

4

Meeting & call summaries

Automatic notes, action items, and follow-ups after every call, filed against the right customer record.

5

Reporting & insight

A plain-language weekly readout of sales, leads, and cash position, assembled from the systems you already have.

6

Integration into existing tools

Everything above lives inside your current CRM, inbox, and calendar. No new dashboard for your team to remember to open.
