the bigger picture

Adoption is nearly universal. Results are not.

The numbers below describe businesses of every size. The pattern holds at ten employees as well as ten thousand.

88%

of organizations now use AI in at least one business function

3 in 4

knowledge workers already use AI at work, most of them with tools they brought in themselves

~4 in 10

can point to a measurable effect on profit. The rest are still running pilots.

The advantage isn't in having AI. It's in having it wired to the work.
