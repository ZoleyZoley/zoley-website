case study

What a first build looks like in practice.

12+ hrs
returned to the team

A home-services company answering the same 6 questions, hundreds of times a month by hand.

Hours, service area, rough pricing, availability. Every inquiry was answered manually, often hours later, and a share of them went cold in the meantime. We documented the real answers, built an assistant that handles first-line questions on the site and by text, and set it to book a call or hand off to a person when a job needed judgment. Response time dropped from hours to seconds, and the team got its afternoons back.

"We used to open up in the morning to a pile of texts we'd let sit overnight. Now the system picks them up right away, answers the simple stuff, and flags anything that actually needs one of us. We're not starting every day behind anymore."

— Sasha D., Med Spa Practice Owner
