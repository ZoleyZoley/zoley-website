A growing service business, chasing its own paperwork.

Leads came in through the website and sat until someone got back to a desk. Invoices were written up at month-end and chased by phone. We built three connected flows: an instant personal reply and CRM entry for every inquiry, a follow-up sequence until the lead booked or declined, and invoicing generated from the job record with automatic reminders. The owner now reads one Monday-morning summary instead of managing the process by hand.
