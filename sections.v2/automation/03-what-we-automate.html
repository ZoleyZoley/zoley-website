What we automate, and what it replaces.

Every flow is built inside the tools you already have, or with new tools we'll show you when one would serve you better. Documented and handed over either way.

1

Lead capture & follow-up

Every inquiry gets a personal reply within minutes, is logged to your CRM, and is followed up on a schedule until it books or opts out.

Replaces: checking the inbox between jobs and hoping nothing slipped.
2

Scheduling & reminders

Bookings, confirmations, reminders, and reschedules handled end-to-end, with your calendar as the single source of truth.

Replaces: the three-email back-and-forth to confirm a time.
3

Invoicing & collections

Invoices generated from the job record, sent on completion, and chased politely on a set cadence until paid.

Replaces: month-end invoicing sessions and awkward reminder calls.
4

Data entry & sync

Information entered once and pushed everywhere it needs to be: CRM, accounting, calendar, spreadsheet.

Replaces: re-typing the same customer details into four systems.
5

Reporting & dashboards

One weekly view of leads, bookings, revenue, and outstanding invoices, assembled automatically and sent to you.

Replaces: digging through five tools to answer "how did we do this week?"
6

Onboarding & handoffs

New client or new hire: the welcome sequence, documents, and access requests fire automatically the moment a deal closes.

Replaces: the checklist that lives in someone's head.
