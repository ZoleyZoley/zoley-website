What happens after you hit send.

01

We read it properly

Your answers go to a person, not a queue. If something's unclear we'll email one question, not a calendar link.

02

You get the plan in writing

What we'd do first, what it includes, what it costs, and what it should return. Written so you can forward it to a partner or an accountant.

03

You decide

Take it, ask questions, compare it, or shelve it. We'll check in once. After that, the next move is yours.
