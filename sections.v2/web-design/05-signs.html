a quick self-audit

Five signs your site is working against you.

These are the issues we find most often in the first ten minutes of a site audit. If two or more apply, a rebuild usually pays for itself within the year.

  • 01

    It was built years ago, and it looks it.

    Visitors read a dated site as a signal about the business behind it, fairly or not.

  • 02

    You don't turn up when people search for you.

    If the page titles, descriptions, and structure were never set up behind the scenes, search engines have almost nothing to go on. The site is live; it just isn't findable.

  • 03

    It's slow, or it breaks on a phone.

    Most of your visitors are on mobile. If the layout shifts, the buttons are tiny, or it stalls, they're gone.

  • 04

    A stranger can't tell what you do in five seconds.

    Vague headlines and industry jargon send people back to the search results.

  • 05

    There's no obvious next step.

    If the visitor has to hunt for how to book, buy, or reach you, most won't. One clear action per page.
