tracking & reporting

You'll know whether it's working, in numbers.

A website without measurement is a guess with a domain name. Every site we launch ships with analytics configured around the actions that matter to your business, and a monthly summary written in plain language.

These are the six numbers we report on. If one of them isn't moving, that's where the next improvement goes.

Qualified inquiries

Form submissions, calls, and bookings that came from the site, not just raw visits.

Conversion rate

The share of visitors who take the action the page was built for.

Load time

Measured on a mid-range phone over a normal connection, which is how most visitors arrive.

Traffic by source

Search, referrals, ads, and direct, so you know which channel is earning its keep.

Top pages & exits

Where visitors go, and the page they were on when they left.

Search visibility

The terms you appear for and where, tracked month over month.
