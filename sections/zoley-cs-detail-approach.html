the approach

Built around the answers they already knew by heart.

We didn't start with a generic chatbot script. We started by listening to what customers actually asked, then trained an assistant to answer the way [Client Name]'s own team would.

  • 1

    Audited two months of customer messages to find the real, repeat questions.

  • 2

    Built and trained an AI assistant on [Client Name]'s actual answers, tone, and policies.

  • 3

    Connected it to the website and Facebook page so it could answer any time, day or night.

  • 4

    Set up a simple handoff so the team only steps in when a question actually needs a person.
