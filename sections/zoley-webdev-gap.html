Signs your website is working against you, not for you.

  • 01

    It was built years ago and it shows.

    An outdated design makes visitors wonder if you're even still in business.

  • 02

    You can't touch it without calling someone.

    Every small edit turns into a support ticket, a wait, and sometimes a bill.

  • 03

    It's slow, or it breaks on a phone.

    Most of your visitors are on mobile. If it stutters there, they leave.

  • 04

    It doesn't say what you actually do.

    Confusing layout and copy send people right back to Google.

  • 05

    There's no clear next step for a visitor.

    No obvious way to book, buy, or reach you means business walks away.
