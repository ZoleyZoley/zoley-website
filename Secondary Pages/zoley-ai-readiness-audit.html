AI Crawl Access

Whether AI crawlers, like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot, can actually reach and read your site, or a blocked robots.txt is quietly locking them out.

Structured Data

Whether your pages carry the schema markup that turns plain text into facts AI engines can lift and trust, instead of ignore.

Answer-Ready Content

Whether your content answers real questions in plain language AI can quote directly, instead of marketing copy that reads well but answers nothing.

Brand Citations

How often other sites, directories, and reviews mention your business by name, the outside signals AI cross-references before it trusts a source.

AI Engine Visibility

When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Gemini a question your business should answer, whether you actually show up in the response.

Content Freshness

Whether your key pages look current. Stale, unmaintained content quietly slides out of what AI chooses to surface.
