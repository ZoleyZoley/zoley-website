Nine replacements for the barn red

A competitor launched on the same cream-and-terracotta pairing we use, and --barn #b5482d is where the two sites collide. Each option below is shown on the real components that use it, so you're judging the site, not a swatch. Every one carries two readings: how far it sits from sage, which decides whether a green still reads as its own signal, and how far it sits from the espresso logo, which decides whether the accent still echoes the mark. What a swap actually touches

The accent is a variable in the page sources but a literal in the illustrations, so a change is mechanical rather than trivial. All of it happens in main pages/*.v2.html and rebuilds through build-sections.py — no hand-editing of section files.

  • 73--barn: declarations, one per section
  • 256literal #b5482d, mostly SVG strokes
  • 124#7c2f19 button shadows
  • 105rgba(181,72,45,…) glows on dark bands

Worth knowing before you pick

Barn has the weakest contrast of all seven — 4.85:1 as a link on paper, just over the 4.5:1 floor. Every option here is better, so whichever you choose, link and button legibility improves as a side effect.

The accent also partners marigold in a left-to-right gradient on section rules and card tops. That pairing is why the cool options read differently: teal and navy make marigold look chosen, while burgundy and plum sit closer to it.
