the challenge

Buried in the same questions, every single day.

[Client Name] runs a small, busy [industry] business — and every day brought the same handful of questions: hours, pricing, availability, whether they served a certain area. Answering them all by hand was eating into the time that should have gone toward actual clients.

Worse, anything that came in after close just sat there until morning. By the time someone got a reply, they'd often already gone with a competitor who answered first.

the approach

Built around the answers they already knew by heart.

We didn't start with a generic chatbot script. We started by listening to what customers actually asked, then trained an assistant to answer the way [Client Name]'s own team would.

  • 1

    Audited two months of customer messages to find the real, repeat questions.

  • 2

    Built and trained an AI assistant on [Client Name]'s actual answers, tone, and policies.

  • 3

    Connected it to the website and Facebook page so it could answer any time, day or night.

  • 4

    Set up a simple handoff so the team only steps in when a question actually needs a person.

the result

Evenings back, and no one waiting until morning.

Within the first month, the assistant was handling the majority of first-line questions on its own — instantly, and around the clock. The team stopped losing evenings to repeat questions, and customers stopped waiting until morning for a simple answer.
