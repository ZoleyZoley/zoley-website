a note on candor

When we'll tell you not to run ads yet.

Paid advertising amplifies what's already there. If the foundation isn't in place, the money accelerates the problem. We'll say so before we quote anything, and point you to the fix that should come first.

  • ×Your site can't convert the traffic. If the landing experience is slow or unclear, ads just make more people leave faster. Fix the page first; it's cheaper.
  • ×Nobody will answer the lead. Paid leads go cold in minutes. Without a follow-up system in place, you're paying to generate inquiries that expire.
  • ×You don't yet know what a customer is worth. Without a rough lifetime value, there's no way to set a budget or judge a cost per lead. We can help you work it out, but it comes before the campaign.
  • ×The budget can't sustain a real test. A campaign needs enough spend and enough weeks to produce a readable result. If it can't, we'll recommend organic or referral work instead.

tracking & reporting

Six numbers, reported monthly, in plain language.

Ad platforms will show you dozens of metrics. Most of them are there to make the platform look good. We report on the handful that connect spend to revenue, and we explain what each one means for the decision in front of you.

You'll always be able to answer one question from the report: for every dollar we put in, what came back?

Total spend

What went out, by channel and campaign, against the budget we agreed.

Cost per click

What each visit cost. Useful for spotting audience or bidding problems early.

Click-through rate

How often people who saw the ad acted on it. A read on whether the message lands.

Cost per lead

What each inquiry cost. The number the whole campaign is managed against.

Conversion rate

The share of visitors who became leads. The clearest signal on the landing page.

Return on ad spend

Revenue attributed to the campaign divided by what it cost. The number that decides whether we scale.
