a note on candor

Where we'd tell you not to bother.

Part of what you're paying for is a straight answer. These are the situations where we'll recommend a simpler fix, or no AI at all, before we quote you anything.

  • ×Your process isn't written down yet. AI amplifies whatever it's given. If the way you quote a job lives in one person's head, we'll document that first. It's cheaper and it's the real bottleneck.
  • ×The task happens fewer than a few times a week. Below that frequency, the setup rarely pays back. A checklist or a template will do.
  • ×The answer has to be exactly right every time. Legal terms, medical guidance, final pricing. AI can draft these for a person to review, but it shouldn't be the last step.
  • ×The real problem is a plain automation. If a lead isn't getting a reply, the fix is a follow-up sequence, not a language model. We'll say so and point you to the simpler build.
