test before you commit

Find out if people will buy, before you build the thing they'd buy.

A validation test puts a real offer in front of real prospects and measures what they do. It takes about a week, costs a fraction of a full launch, and it's the single most reliable way we know to avoid the most common cause of failure.

"The fastest way to learn is by doing, not by thinking about doing."

Set up a validation test

How a one-week test runs

  1. Day 1–2: the offer. One page describing the product, the price, and a way to say "I want this."
  2. Day 3–6: the traffic. A small, targeted ad spend or direct outreach to the people it's for.
  3. Day 7: the read. Sign-ups, deposits, or replies against a threshold we set in advance.
Clear the threshold and you launch with proof. Miss it and you've saved months.
